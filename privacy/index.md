# Spotted privacy policy

**Last updated:** 7 November 2026 · **Draft for review**

Spotted is a free app for sharing wildlife sightings in Kruger National Park. This policy explains
what personal information Spotted handles, why, where it is kept and what you can do about it. It is
written to meet South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

**Information Officer / privacy contact:** [TO CONFIRM: privacy contact email].

## The short version

- You don't need an email or real name to use Spotted. You get a made-up name like "Dusty Hornbill
  427". Other people only ever see that name.
- Sightings you choose to share are shown to other users with the location rounded to about 50 m.
  Rhino and pangolin sightings are never shown to anyone.
- Reports to rangers (snares, injured animals and similar) are seen only by Spotted moderators and,
  later, SANParks partners. They are never public.
- Trips, your route and where you've been stay on your phone.
- You can delete your account and everything linked to it from the app at any time.
- We don't sell your information, show ads or use tracking for advertising.

**Stays on your phone and is never uploaded:** your trips and trip journal, the "Record my route"
track (only if you turn it on), the inside/outside-the-park samples used to detect trips, your
settings, and the original full-size photos.

**Your location** is used on your phone to place sightings, count down to your gate and predict
Space Station passes. Spotted only uses location in the background if you turn on "Record my
route", and that route stays on your phone.

**The likelihood heatmap** is built from verified sightings that are more than a day old, grouped
into areas of about 5 km². An area only appears once at least 5 sightings from at least 3
different people fall in it. It never includes rhino or pangolin and never shows who saw what.

**Share cards** you create for trips and badges are made on your phone and never contain a
location, map or route.

## How long we keep it

- Anonymous accounts that never upload a sighting or report are deleted automatically after 30
  days.
- Everything else is kept while you have an account, until you delete it.
- Backups kept by our hosting provider are overwritten on their normal schedule.

## Your rights

Under POPIA you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to
delete it, or object to how we use it.

- **Delete everything:** in the app, go to **Profile → Account → Delete my account and data**. This
  deletes your account, handle, sightings, photos, reports, points and badges from our servers, and
  clears the same from your phone. It cannot be undone.
- **Anything else:** email [TO CONFIRM: privacy contact email]. We aim to answer within 30 days.
- If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the **Information
  Regulator (South Africa)**: https://inforegulator.org.za.

## Children

Spotted includes a kids' tracks game that needs no account. Children under 18 should only create
an account or share sightings with a parent's or guardian's permission. [TO CONFIRM with a
reviewer.]

## Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Our database only lets each person read what they are
allowed to (for example, nobody can read another person's reports or exact locations), and
sensitive species are blocked in the database itself, not just in the app.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app before the change applies.
